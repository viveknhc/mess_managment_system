MessDeck User Guide

How to use MessDeck, from adding a new mess to delivering the first meal. Every screen, every button, and what happens when you press it.

MessDeck replaces the notebook, WhatsApp messages and spreadsheets a mess uses to track customers, meal plans, payments and deliveries. Every screenshot in this guide comes from the real app. In this guide a new mess, Shree Sai Mess, is added and set up step by step. Some screens use the demo mess, Annapurna Mess, because it already has a month of data.

Jump to your part of the guide:

Super adminYou run MessDeck. You add messes, choose their plan and features, and record what they pay you. Mess ownerRuns one mess: customers, plans, payments, staff and deliveries. ManagerSame daily work as the owner. Only the owner can change settings or cancel the MessDeck plan. Delivery staffToday's deliveries on their phone. One tap to mark delivered. Kitchen staffHow many of each meal to cook today. CustomerTheir plan, meals and payments. Can skip a meal or pause.

1Logging in

Everyone uses the same login page. MessDeck sends each person to their own screens based on who they are.

  1. Open the MessDeck link in any browser, on a phone or computer.
  2. Type your Username and Password, then press Sign in.
The super admin lands on Platform Admin. Owners and managers land on their mess Dashboard. Delivery and kitchen staff get their phone screen. Customers get My Mess. You stay logged in for up to 7 days on that device.

Forgot your password?

  1. On the login page, press Forgot password?
  2. Type your username or your email address and press Send reset link.
  3. Open the email from MessDeck. It also reminds you of your username. Press the link, type a new password twice, and press Set new password.
The link works once and only for 1 hour. After you set the new password you are logged out on every other phone or computer, so nobody else stays logged in with the old one.
A reset needs an email address on your account. If you don't have one, ask your mess owner to add it (owners: ask MessDeck support). The new password must be at least 8 characters, not only numbers, and not a common password.

Messages and popups you'll see everywhere

  • Green message at the top (for example "Customer added", "Payment of ₹2,400 recorded"): what you did worked. It disappears by itself.
  • Red message at the top: something didn't work. It says why, for example "Amount exceeds pending amount of ₹300".
  • Popup asking "Are you sure?" before anything hard to undo: cancelling, suspending, deactivating, blocking or skipping. Press the coloured button to go ahead, or Cancel to stop. On phones it slides up from the bottom.
  • Undo appears on some messages, for example after marking a meal delivered. Press it to take the action back.

MessDeck plans and features

Plans are what a mess pays you each month. Each plan has a monthly price, a one-time setup fee, a reactivation fee (charged if a mess comes back more than 3 months after its last paid month), limits on customers and staff, and the features it includes.

The features you can switch on or off are:

  • Customer portal: customers can log in, see their meals, pay, skip and pause.
  • Online payments: customers pay in the app through the mess's own Razorpay account.
  • Reports: charts and reports for the owner.

Everything else (customers, plans, subscriptions, payments, deliveries, staff) is always included.

When you change a plan's features, every mess on that plan gets the change the next time its users open or reload the app.

Adding a new mess

  1. Go to Businesses and press Add Business.
  2. Fill in the mess's name, phone, email and address.
  3. Under Owner login, fill in the owner's name, phone and email, then choose a username and a password (at least 8 characters). Give these to the owner.
  4. Press Create Business.
  • The mess and its owner login are created straight away. The owner can log in now.
  • Four meals are added for them: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Tiffin.
  • The mess starts as Awaiting first payment on the standard Monthly Plan. If nothing is paid within 7 days, it is suspended automatically.

Recording a mess's payment to you

When a mess pays you by UPI, cash or bank transfer:

  1. In Businesses, press Record Payment on that mess.
  2. The amount due is already filled in (the first payment is setup fee + first month). Choose the method, add the UPI or bank reference, and press Record Payment.
The mess becomes Active and is paid for one month. The next monthly payment is due the day after "Paid until". The amount must match what is due, so mistakes are caught. Messes can also pay you online from their own Billing page, which records it automatically.

Customizing one mess

Every mess runs on the same software, but each can work a little differently. Press Customize on a mess (in Businesses or Mess Details).

  • Features: each one says Follow plan (what its plan gives), On for this mess or Off for this mess. Use this to give one mess an extra feature, or take one away, without changing its plan.
  • Workflows → Meal skip: what happens when a customer skips a meal.
    • No adjustment (default): the skipped meal is lost.
    • Extend end date: the subscription ends one day later and the meal is delivered then.
Press Save. The change applies to this mess only, and its users see it after they reload. Every change is recorded in the mess's activity log with your name. Owners see the skip rule on their Settings page but cannot change it.

Mess details: everything about one mess

Open Mess Details. The list on the left shows every mess (type to search). Click one to see everything about it on the right. Clicking a mess's name in Businesses opens the same page.

Features marked Changed for this mess differ from the plan. The note in brackets shows what the plan alone would give.

All payments to MessDeck

3Mess owner: first login

This part is for owners and managers. Your login details come from MessDeck. On your first login the dashboard is empty. Set up your mess in this order.

Setup checklist (about 15 minutes)
  1. Check your business profile and settings
  2. Check your meals, then add your meal plans
  3. Add your customers
  4. Start a subscription for each customer
  5. Record what each customer paid
  6. Add logins for your delivery and kitchen staff

Business profile and settings

Business Profile shows your mess name, phone, email, address and logo. Press Edit to change them, or upload a logo.

Settings controls how your mess works. Change what you need and press Save Settings.

  • When a meal is skipped: set by MessDeck for your mess (see Customizing). Contact support to change it.
  • Min / Max Pause Days: the shortest and longest pause a customer can ask for.
  • Notification preferences: which events alert you (payments received, subscriptions ending soon, deliveries).
  • Online payments: if your plan includes it, paste your Razorpay Key ID and Key Secret and tick Customers can pay online. Money goes straight to your Razorpay account. The secret is stored encrypted and never shown again.
Only the owner can save Settings. Managers can see them.

Meals and meal plans

Meals are the kinds of meal you serve. Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Tiffin are already there. Use Add Meal for more, Edit to rename, or Deactivate to stop offering one (you'll be asked to confirm; you can activate it again any time).

Plans are what customers buy, for example "Monthly Lunch, 30 days, 30 meals, ₹2,400".

  1. Go to Plans and press Add Plan.
  2. Give it a name, choose the meal, and set the number of Days it lasts, the number of Meals and the Price.
  3. Tick Allow skip if customers may skip single meals, and Allow pause if they may pause for some days.
  4. Press Create.
Changing a plan's price later doesn't change subscriptions that already started; they keep the price they were sold at.

Adding customers

  1. Go to Customers and press Add Customer.
  2. Fill in name and phone (needed), plus email, address and location. The address is what delivery staff see.
  3. Press Create.
Two customers can't share the same phone number; MessDeck warns you. Have many customers already? Use Import CSV on the Customers page to upload them all from a CSV file (download the template first).

Click any customer to open their page, where you can Edit their details, Set Inactive, or Block them (asks you to confirm).

Starting a subscription

A subscription connects a customer to a plan from a start date.

  1. Go to Subscriptions and press New Subscription.
  2. Choose the Customer and the Plan, and pick the Start Date (today or later).
  3. Press Create Subscription.
The subscription is PENDING: it waits for payment and no meals are scheduled yet. The end date is worked out from the plan's number of days. A customer can't have two subscriptions to the same plan at the same time, but can have different plans together (for example lunch and dinner).

Recording a customer's payment

  1. Go to Customer Payments and press Record Payment (also on the Dashboard under Quick Actions).
  2. Choose the subscription, type the amount, choose how they paid (cash, UPI, card or bank transfer), and add the UPI reference if there is one.
  3. Press Record Payment.
  • Paid in full: the subscription turns ACTIVE, every meal of the plan is scheduled for delivery, and you get a notification.
  • Paid part: it stays PENDING and shows what is still due. Record the rest later.
  • MessDeck won't accept more than what is due.

Staff logins

  1. Go to Staff and press Add Staff.
  2. Choose a username, type their full name, pick the Role (Manager, Delivery Staff or Kitchen Staff) and set a password.
  3. Press Create and give them their username and password.
Give every person their own login and never share yours. Add an email for each person so they can reset their own password.
Customer logins for the customer app are created by MessDeck support for now. Ask support if you want your customers to use it.

Your daily routine

  1. Open the Dashboard: customers, active subscriptions, today's money in, and money still owed.
  2. Look at Expiring Subscriptions: who ends today, within 3 days or this week, and who is waiting for payment. Call them about renewing.
  3. Look at Today's Deliveries: how many are delivered and pending, and how many of each meal.
  4. Press the bell at the top for alerts (payments received, subscriptions activated or ending).

On the Subscriptions page, the tabs filter by status. The buttons on each row:

ButtonWhat happens
PauseStops deliveries from today until you press Resume. When you resume, the end date moves out by the meals that were paused, so the customer loses nothing.
ResumeStarts deliveries again from today.
CancelEnds the subscription for good. Asks you to confirm.
RenewOn expired or cancelled subscriptions: makes a new Pending subscription to the same plan. Record the payment to start it.

Deliveries

Deliveries lists every meal due today with the customer's phone and address. Your delivery staff usually update these from their phones, but you can too:

  • Delivered: marks it done. An Undo appears for a few seconds.
  • Not Delivered: asks for a reason (customer not home, door locked…).
  • Skip: the customer doesn't want today's meal. Asks you to confirm. Whether the subscription is extended depends on your mess's skip rule.

Reports

If your plan includes Reports, Reports shows money collected over time, subscriptions by status, meals served, and customer and payment lists. Pick a date range at the top.

Your MessDeck bill

MessDeck Billing is what your mess pays MessDeck. It has nothing to do with your customers' payments. It shows your plan, the date you're paid until, and the next amount due.

  • Pay by UPI or cash and press Send payment details on WhatsApp. Support updates your account the same day. If online payment is set up, you can also pay here directly.
  • If a payment is late, you have 7 days before your account is suspended.
  • Cancel monthly plan (owner only): you keep using MessDeck until the end of your paid month. The setup fee is not refunded.

4Delivery staff

Made for your phone. Log in with the username and password from your mess owner.

  • The blue box shows how many meals are left and how many are done. The round button refreshes the list.
  • To deliver shows what is still left. Done shows what you've finished.
  • Type in the search box to find a customer by name, address or meal.
  • Each card shows the customer, the meal and the address. Call phones them. Directions opens Google Maps.

Delivered: one tap

Hand over the meal, then press the green Delivered button.

The card moves to Done with the time, and the count goes down. Pressed it by mistake? Press Undo on the message, or on the card in the Done tab. Undo works for 10 minutes. After that, ask your owner to correct it.

Couldn't deliver?

  1. Press Not delivered.
  2. Tap a reason (Customer not home, Door locked, Not answering phone, Wrong address, Customer refused) or type your own.
  3. Press Mark not delivered.
The list refreshes by itself when you come back to the app, so new customers added by the owner show up.

6Customers

If your mess gives you a login, you can check your plan and manage your meals from your phone.

Skip a meal

  1. On My Subscription, press Skip Meal.
  2. Pick the date (empty means today) and, if you like, a reason. Press Skip Meal.
That day's meal is not delivered. Depending on your mess, your plan may end one day later so you still get the meal. You can't skip a past date, and only plans that allow skipping show this button.

Pause

  1. Press Pause Subscription.
  2. Pick From and, if you know it, To. Press Pause.
No meals come during the pause. When it ends, your plan's end date moves out by the meals you missed. If the pause starts later, it is scheduled and you keep getting meals until then. Your mess sets the shortest and longest pause allowed.

Meals, payments and profile

When your plan ends, press Renew to ask for another month. Then pay your mess (or pay online if your mess offers it); the new plan starts once it's paid.

7What happens automatically

Every night just after midnight, MessDeck does these jobs by itself:

JobWhat it does
MessDeck billingMarks messes whose monthly fee is due as Payment overdue, and suspends them 7 days later if still unpaid.
Ending subscriptionsActive subscriptions past their end date become EXPIRED.
PausesStarts pauses that begin today and ends pauses that are over (moving the end date out).
DeliveriesMakes sure tomorrow's deliveries are in the list.
Expiry alertsEmails the owner a list of subscriptions ending soon, and tells customers whose plan is ending (if they have an email and the owner turned this on).

The full journey of one customer:

Customer added→Subscription PENDING→Paid in full→ACTIVE + meals scheduled→Delivered daily→End date passes → EXPIRED→Renew → new PENDING

9Questions and problems

I can't see Reports, or customers can't log in.
That feature isn't part of your MessDeck plan. Contact MessDeck support to add it.
The page says my account is suspended.
Your MessDeck bill is unpaid. Open MessDeck Billing, pay the amount shown, and send the payment details on WhatsApp.
A subscription stays Pending.
It isn't fully paid yet. Check the amount pending and record the rest under Customer Payments.
A customer isn't in today's deliveries.
Check their subscription: it may be Pending (not paid), Paused, Expired, or they skipped today.
A delivery was marked by mistake.
The delivery person can press Undo within 10 minutes. After that, contact your owner.
I didn't get the password reset email.
Check spam. The account needs an email address; ask your owner (or MessDeck support) to add one, then try again.
A change MessDeck made for my mess isn't showing.
Reload the page, or log out and back in.
