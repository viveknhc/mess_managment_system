MessDeck Owner Guide

Everything covered in your 30-minute walkthrough, on one page. Keep it handy.

1. Your daily routine (5 minutes)

  1. Open Dashboard. You'll see today's meals, deliveries, money collected and pending dues.
  2. Check the bell icon for alerts. Subscriptions ending in the next 3 days show up here, and you also get a daily email if your email is on your profile.
  3. Kitchen staff open Today's Meals on their phone to see how many of each meal to cook.
  4. Delivery staff open Today's Deliveries and mark each one Delivered or Not delivered.
  5. Record any money you received in Payments.

2. Adding a new customer

  1. Customers → Add Customer. Name and phone are required.
  2. Subscriptions → create a subscription for the customer, choosing a plan and start date. It starts as Pending.
  3. Payments → record the payment. When the full amount is paid, the subscription becomes Active and the customer's deliveries are scheduled automatically.

Many customers at once: Customers → Import CSV. Download the template, fill it in Excel or Google Sheets (up to 100 rows per file), save as CSV and upload. If any row has a problem, nothing is imported and you'll see which rows to fix.

3. Meals and plans

  • Meals: your meal types. Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Tiffin are added for you. Add or rename as you like.
  • Plans: what you sell, e.g. "Lunch, 30 days, ₹2,400". Each plan has a meal, a duration and a price.

4. Skip and pause

  • Skip a meal: from Deliveries, or the customer skips it from their own login. The delivery is taken off the list.
  • Pause: in Subscriptions, pause a customer who is away, and resume when they're back. Customers can also request a pause for a date range from their login. Deliveries for paused days are removed from the list and come back when the pause ends.
  • How skipped days are adjusted (extend the end date, give a meal credit, or no change) is set in Settings.

5. Staff and customer logins

WhoWhat they see
Owner (you)Everything, including Billing and Settings

Add staff in Staff. Give each person their own login, and never share yours.

6. Reports

Reports shows your customers, subscriptions, revenue, meal counts and payments. Use it at month end to see collections and pending dues.

7. Your MessDeck bill

  • ₹500 per month. Your first payment was ₹3,000 (₹2,500 one-time setup + ₹500 for the first month).
  • Billing shows the date you're paid until and what's due next.
  • Pay by UPI or cash, then message us on WhatsApp so we can record it.
  • If a payment is more than 7 days late, the account is suspended until it's paid. Your data is kept safe.
  • You can cancel any time from Billing. Access continues until the paid month ends. If you come back after more than 3 months, a ₹1,000 reactivation fee applies.

8. Getting help

Open Help & Support in the app for our WhatsApp number and email. Your data is backed up every day and is private to your business.
