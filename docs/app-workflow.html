Annapurna Mess

A simple guide to how the app works — for everyone on the team

1 What does this app do?
It replaces your notebooks, WhatsApp messages, and spreadsheets with one simple system.

Annapurna Mess is a digital management system for mess / tiffin / food subscription businesses. Instead of tracking customers in notebooks and payments on paper, everything is in one place.

👥

Customer Records

Name, phone, address — all searchable

📋

Meal Plans

Monthly Lunch, Weekly Breakfast — any plan you create

💳

Payment Tracking

Cash, UPI, bank transfer — who paid, who hasn't

🚚

Daily Deliveries

Today's delivery list, mark delivered or not

⏰

Expiry Alerts

Know which subscriptions are about to expire

📊

Reports

Revenue, meal counts, customer stats at a glance

2 Who uses it? (User Roles)
There are 5 types of users. Each sees only what they need.
👑
Owner
The mess business owner (e.g., Ramesh)
  • See dashboard with all business stats
  • Add / edit / remove customers
  • Create meal plans and pricing
  • Create subscriptions for customers
  • Record payments (cash, UPI, etc.)
  • View all deliveries and mark status
  • View reports and revenue charts
  • Add staff and manage their roles
  • Change business settings
📝
Manager
Trusted assistant (e.g., Priya)
  • Everything the Owner can do
  • Except: cannot change business settings
  • Except: cannot add/remove staff
  • Helps owner with daily operations
  • Can record payments, manage customers
  • Can create subscriptions
🛵
Delivery Staff
The person delivering food (e.g., Vikram)
  • See today's delivery list with addresses
  • Mark each delivery as "Delivered"
  • Mark as "Not Delivered" with a note
  • Cannot see payments or revenue
  • Cannot add/change customers or plans
👨‍🍳
Kitchen Staff
The cook / kitchen team (e.g., Sunita)
  • See how many meals to prepare today
  • Breakdown by meal type (Lunch, Dinner, etc.)
  • Cannot see customer details
  • Cannot see payments or subscriptions
  • Simple, focused view
🙋
Customer
A person who has subscribed to meals (e.g., Rahul)
  • See their own subscription details
  • See how many days are remaining
  • See their payment history
  • See their delivery history
  • Skip a future meal ("I won't be home tomorrow")
  • Cannot see other customers' data

Owner vs Manager — What's the difference?

Think of the Manager as a "second-in-command." They can handle all daily work — but only the Owner can change business settings, add new staff, or make big decisions.

3 How does the app work? (Main Workflow)
This is the journey from a new customer joining to daily food delivery.
Owner
1
Add a New Customer
Enter customer's name, phone, address. They get a unique code (CUST-0001).
Customer is saved in the system
Owner
2
Choose a Meal Plan
Pick a plan like "Monthly Lunch - 30 days - Rs.2,500" for the customer.
Subscription created with status: PENDING
Owner
3
Record the Payment
Customer pays Rs.2,500 via Cash / UPI / Bank Transfer. Owner records it in the app.
Payment saved. If fully paid, subscription becomes ACTIVE automatically!
System
4
Delivery Schedule Generated
The app automatically creates a delivery entry for every day of the subscription (e.g., 30 days = 30 deliveries).
Today's delivery appears in the delivery staff's list
Delivery
5
Deliver the Food
Delivery staff sees the customer's name, address, and meal type. Delivers the food and taps "Delivered" in the app.
Delivery marked as DELIVERED with timestamp
Kitchen
6
Kitchen Prepares Meals
Kitchen staff opens the app and sees: "Today: 15 Lunch, 8 Dinner, 5 Breakfast". They know exactly how much to cook.
Customer
7
Customer Manages Their Meals
Customer opens the portal and can: check remaining days, view payment receipts, skip tomorrow's meal, or pause for a few days.
Skipped meals are removed from delivery list
System
8
Expiry Alert
When a subscription is about to expire (3 days left), the system alerts the owner on the dashboard. If it expires, the nightly job marks it EXPIRED.
Owner can contact the customer to renew
Owner
9
Renew the Subscription
Customer wants to continue. Owner creates a renewal — a new subscription linked to the old one. Record the new payment. The cycle continues!
New subscription ACTIVE, new deliveries generated

5 A Typical Day
Here's what each person does every day.
Morning — Owner / Manager
Check the Dashboard
  • Open the app and see today's stats
  • Check "Expiring" subscriptions — call those customers
  • Record any new payments received
  • Add new customers if any walked in
  • Create subscriptions for new sign-ups
Morning — Kitchen Staff
Check Meal Counts
  • Open the app → Kitchen view
  • See: "Lunch: 18, Dinner: 12, Breakfast: 5"
  • Prepare the right quantities
Delivery Time — Delivery Staff
Deliver and Mark
  • Open the app → Today's Deliveries
  • See customer name, address, meal type
  • Deliver the food
  • Tap "Delivered" or "Not Delivered"
  • Add a note if needed
Evening — Owner
Review the Day
  • Check how many deliveries were completed
  • View today's revenue / collections
  • Check notifications for any issues
  • Plan for tomorrow
